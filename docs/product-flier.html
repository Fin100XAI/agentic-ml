The problem

Good data, indefensible decisions.

The numbers a department needs already exist - but they arrive as workbooks with title banners, merged headers, totals mixed into the rows, the same district spelled four ways, and figures stored as text. By the time a decision is taken, nobody can show how the number was reached. The Workbench closes that gap: it makes the preparation visible, the reasoning traceable, and the officer the one who decides.

What it does

Three capabilities, one workbench.

Preparation is the front door. After that the officer picks the direction, and can switch at any point without re-uploading anything.

Prepare

Data Prep Studio

The agents read the files before asking anything: banner rows, two-row merged headers, units hidden in a note, totals, footnotes, duplicate keys. Then they ask only what they could not work out - and you approve a schema contract naming every column, its type, its role and its unit. Out comes a clean table with a data dictionary a department can share.

Understand & ask

Answers in seconds

The agents propose the questions worth asking, you tick and reword them, and only then do they run. Results come back charted, mapped and explained in plain language - then you ask your own questions, each shown as a step-by-step plan before it executes.

Predict & decide

Models with cover

For questions about what will happen, the right method is recommended and explained, risks in the data are flagged, and the model is checked on data it never saw. You receive a decision brief with a trust rating that a critic agent has already reviewed line by line.

Why it can be trusted

Built for the day someone asks how you got that number.

Privacy before analysis

Mobile numbers, Aadhaar-like IDs, PAN, names and addresses are detected and masked or dropped before any analysis or AI call sees a single row.

Numbers computed, never guessed

Every figure is calculated deterministically in code. The AI only explains and phrases - it cannot invent a number, and each output is badged AI or rule-based.

An officer approves every step

Agents propose; nothing runs without approval - data fixes, the questions, the method, the settings, the thresholds. Authority stays with the human.

An audit trail fit for review

Every upload, AI call, approval, decline, training job and export is logged, with the original file kept read-only under a content fingerprint.

The workforce

Seventeen agents. One rule.

Each one proposes or phrases. None computes a number, and none acts alone. Every agent has a rule-based fallback, so the platform keeps working with no AI connection at all - outputs are simply badged rule-based instead of AI.
